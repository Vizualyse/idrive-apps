# native/touch

A version is frozen once a newer one exists. A component moves to a new
one with `node platform/platform.js use native/touch <version>`.

## 1.0.0

`touch.c` / `touch.h`, the centre display's touches read passively, as
they were on main at 68aa156: from vizualyse-moonlight and
vizualyse-chromium (and vizualyse-steam, from 0.1.5).
